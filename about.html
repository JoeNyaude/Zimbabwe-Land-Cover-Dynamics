ZIMLCD is a research prototype for exploring land-cover patterns and image change across selected areas of Zimbabwe.

How it works

Dated Esri Wayback satellite images are fetched for the selected years and analyzed with the project's local CNN and change-detection models.

  • Land cover: a scene-level class prediction for each complete snapshot.
  • Change detection: a U-Net model's pixel-level change probability mask and the CNN's paired snapshot classes.
  • Reports: a summary of the most recent analysis in the current session.

The local models are not independently validated for Esri Wayback imagery. Treat their output as estimates and compare important results with trusted local reference data.
